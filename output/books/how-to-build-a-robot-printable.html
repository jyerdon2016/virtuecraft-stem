Page 1: Planning & Design

Virtue: Patience
📖 Scene Exploration & Virtue Lesson:

A curious seven-year-old child sits focused at a sunlit wooden workbench, carefully sketching the initial robot blueprint with a pencil and clear plastic ruler. Crumpled paper ideas dot the desk alongside colorful markers, showing that good engineering takes steady practice and careful planning. Soft morning light streams through the window, highlighting three hidden 'Cross-my-heart' shapes disguised seamlessly among the blueprint cloud bubbles and pencil jar patterns. The child smiles thoughtfully while measuring each arm component, demonstrating the virtue of Patience by taking time to plan before building. Resting on the corner shelf, a cheerful toy helper bot stands ready, inspiring the young builder to turn dreams into real mechanical designs.

Page 3: Core Component Assembly

Virtue: Gentleness
📖 Scene Exploration & Virtue Lesson:

Tucked into the window foliage outside and the wire loops on the table, three hidden 'Cross-my-heart' shapes wait to be discovered by keen young eyes. The scene teaches the virtue of Gentleness, demonstrating how delicate microchips and fragile pins require calm, careful handling to prevent bending. Outside the sunny window, birds chirp peacefully as the child tests how the sensor detects warm temperature changes in the room.
